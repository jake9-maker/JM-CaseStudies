- **The problem:** A legacy admin portal everyone tolerated: shared logins meant no one was accountable for changes, and unclear fields, dead options, and no guidance caused constant errors.

## The problem

The old portal was slow and confusing, but it worked, and no one had time to fix it. People had learned to work around it.

My research showed it was doing real damage. Shared logins meant no one knew who changed what. Unclear required fields caused errors that had to be fixed later. Legacy and non-functional options cluttered every screen. People felt overwhelmed and anxious using it.

**The insight:** don't just modernize it. Rebuild from first principles: remove everything legacy, guide every step, and make accountability visible.

![Admin Portal User Journey](/images/adminportal-userjourney.png)

## Three decisions that drove the outcome

### 1. Remove the legacy

I audited every field and option against how people actually work today. Anything outdated or broken came out, and sensible defaults replaced manual input wherever possible.

**Result:** Workflows became direct paths instead of mazes. Fewer choices meant fewer errors, and common tasks got noticeably faster.

![Admin Portal Configuration](/images/adminportal-configuration.png)

### 2. Guide every step

I added inline prompts explaining what each field needs and why, real-time validation that flags errors immediately instead of on submit, and a logical field order.

**Result:** People stopped guessing. Errors and support questions dropped because the interface explained itself.

![Admin Portal User Goals](/images/adminportal-user-goals.png)

### 3. Make accountability visible

I replaced shared logins with individual accounts, logged every change, and designed a dashboard showing recent activity, outstanding tasks, and key metrics.

**Result:** Compliance got the audit trail it was missing ("Activity logs solve our audit trail problem"), and teams gained real-time visibility into operations.

![Admin Portal Dashboard](/images/adminportal-dashboard.png)

## What changed

![Admin Portal Sitemap](/images/adminportal-sitemap.png)

- **For users:** "Finally clear, I know what I'm doing" and "This actually helps me work faster."
- **For the team:** Less time answering portal questions, more time on higher-value work.
- **For the company:** The accountability approach is being extended to other internal tools.

## What I took away

Tools people "tolerate" quietly cost a lot. The friction compounds into errors and frustration every day.

Now I look for internal tools that are tolerated rather than loved. Because they're used constantly, fixing them has outsized impact.
